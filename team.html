Audrey Leroux

Associate Professor · Quantitative Psychology Area Director

Click to read bio

Dr. Audrey Leroux is an Associate Professor in the School of Psychological and Brain Sciences at the Georgia Institute of Technology, where she also serves as the Quantitative Psychology area director. Previously, she was an Associate Professor of Research, Measurement, and Statistics in the Department of Educational Policy Studies at Georgia State University. She received her B.S. in Mathematics and Ph.D. in Quantitative Methods from The University of Texas at Austin.

Her research program focuses in two areas: multilevel modeling and computerized adaptive testing, both of which aim to solve methodological dilemmas encountered by applied researchers in the education field as well as more generally in the social and behavioral sciences. Her multilevel modeling research examines the impacts of different multilevel models for handling complex data structures such as individual mobility across clusters. Her computerized adaptive testing research concentrates on novel procedures within item exposure controls and stopping rules. Many of her research collaborations have been federally funded by the National Science Foundation, Institute of Education Sciences, and Department of Health and Human Services.

Jie Yee Lim

Jie Yee Lim is a Ph.D. student advised by Dr. Audrey Leroux. She received her B.S. in Psychology from Chung Yuan Christian University in Taiwan and her M.S.Ed. in Statistics, Measurement, Assessment and Research Technology from the University of Pennsylvania. Her research focuses on psychometrics and quantitative methods, including item response theory (IRT), multilevel modeling, and simulation studies. In her free time, she loves traveling and baking.
