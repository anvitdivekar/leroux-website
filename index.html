Our Research

Multilevel Modeling

We develop and compare statistical methods for complex data structures — including cross-classified and multiple-membership designs — where individuals move across or belong to multiple groups simultaneously. Our goal is to improve inference and applied decision-making in social and behavioral research.

Psychological Measurement

Our measurement research focuses on computerized adaptive testing (CAT), examining stopping rules and item exposure control procedures through simulation studies. CAT can substantially reduce test length while maintaining precision, depending on item pool design and selection criteria.
