Undergraduate Applicants

We are always interested in working with motivated undergraduates who are curious about quantitative psychology, statistics, and psychological measurement. Research experience in our lab offers hands-on exposure to simulation studies, statistical programming, and the scientific publication process.

The following qualities are helpful but not required:

  • Interest in statistics, research methods, or data science
  • Basic experience with R, or a genuine willingness to learn
  • Reliability, strong communication, and the ability to follow through on commitments
  • Comfort working independently on open-ended tasks

Relevant coursework (e.g., statistics, psychology research methods) is always a plus, but we care more about motivation and work ethic than prior formal training.

To apply, please send an email to lab-email@gatech.edu with the following:

  • A brief introduction about yourself
  • A short statement of interest (why this lab, what you hope to gain)
  • Your CV or resume
  • Your typical weekly availability

Please use the subject line:

Undergraduate Research – [Your Name]

PhD Applicants

PhD students in our lab are admitted through the Georgia Tech School of Psychology, not directly into the lab. If you are interested in working with Dr. Leroux as your advisor, here is how to proceed:

  1. Apply to the Georgia Tech School of Psychology graduate program through the official admissions portal. Be sure to mention the Leroux Lab and your specific research interests in your personal statement.
  2. Review current lab projects and publications so you can speak concretely to how your interests align with our work in multilevel modeling and/or psychological measurement.
  3. Check the Georgia Tech School of Psychology website for official application deadlines and requirements.

You are also welcome (but not required) to reach out by email before applying. If you do, please include:

  • A brief introduction about yourself and your research background
  • A statement of how your interests align with the lab's research
  • Your CV
  • An optional writing sample (e.g., a paper or report you are proud of)

Please use the subject line:

Prospective PhD Applicant – [Your Name]
